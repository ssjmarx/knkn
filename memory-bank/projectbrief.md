# Project Brief — KON KON

> Distilled identity. Full canon: `design.md` (game design, lock v1.0) and `syllabus.md` (course roadmap). This file answers "what is this?"; details live in the canon files.

## What KON KON is

**KON KON** (working title — the title itself is open decision ✎ #1: *KON KON / Nine Stones / The Long Hum / Commonweal*) is a **GBC-energy monster-taming JRPG** for the web:

- **One bond, nine forms.** You never catch monsters. You have a single partner — Kon Kon, a fox spirit — who shape-shifts into nine forms (base + eight planetary attunements). The collection instinct is retargeted from bodies to forms, moves, and mastery.
- **Eight planetary stones, any order.** Sol, Luna, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Terra — earn them in whatever order you choose; wilds never scale, so no route is punished.
- **Post-scarcity Americana.** "The Commonweal" (✎ name sign-off pending), a rural countryside of towns grown like rings around eight quietly planted stones, the World-Tree (the Axis) visible on the horizon from every road.
- **Battles are tests, not war.** Full HP between every fight; no attrition; defeat means *"The test is over."* and a free retry — a passerby (often the town dog) walks you home. Nothing is caught; nothing is harmed.

## The six pillars

1. **One bond, nine forms** — the collection instinct retargeted from bodies to forms, moves, and mastery
2. **Freedom with a pulse** — stones in any order, wilds never scale, the world runs on its own calendar whether you participate or not
3. **The test, not the war** — full HP between fights; battles are mediation
4. **Two actors, one body** — an untargetable Channeler (support) + the creature (attacks/setup) as a two-character JRPG
5. **Mastery is the journey** — frequent level-ups, visible tails, a completion track that *is* the fox's body
6. **Country & cosmos** — planetary myth rooted in rural Americana

## Tone rules

- Utopian conflict is always **imbalance** — never money, never cruelty on screen.
- Comedy rationed: exactly **three magical-girl homage beats**.
- The thesis (the Matriarch's answer): **the mortal world is worth staying for.**

## Design rulings that shape everything (lock v1.0)

- **Tails = mastered stones** (Mastery 255 each), not stones carried. Base fox = 1 tail; all eight mastered = nine tails.
- **The ascent is sequel-only.** This game ends at the Sun Road's base, never on the stairs.
- Wilds **never scale**; guardians scale with stones attuned (L ≈ 15 + 20 × stones attuned).
- Balance invariant: the **Rule of 500** — same-level neutral mirror hits-to-KO ≈ 500 ÷ Power (design targets Power 60–125; bosses 10–20 turns; trash 2–4).
- The full systems canon — forms/stats, 67 moves & 92 instances, the battle system (Wane, 29 rites, Foxfire Split, packs), Powers, the 8×8 type chart, reputation tiers, overworld verbs, story spine, cast — is `design.md` §5–§13.

## Scope & success

The grading rubric is the **Scope Ledger** (`design.md` §14): 9 battle + 9 overworld form sprites, ~15 launch-zone wilds, ~25 named humans, 67 combat effects (~90 SFX), 12+ settlements, 8 stone quests in 8 distinct verbs (clock race, mirror dungeon, chase, dance, escort, weather-timing, time-loop puzzle, root descent), 4 Purist gates, 1 raid (6 set-pieces), the Heart sequence, ~14 NPC arcs, ~35–40 music tracks. Systems build order: battle loop → Channeler/Wane → transformation → mastery → packs → split → clock/calendar → schedules → Greeter dogs → reputation/flags → Ledger.
